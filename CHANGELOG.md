# Changelog

The big steps on the way to opening, newest first. Small fixes in between aren't listed.

## 0.7: The catalogue · November 2026

Every print Cor Photos makes now has its own place, laid out like a shop at
[corphotos.com/prints](https://corphotos.com/prints).

- **Four lines of prints.** The Classics, the everyday prints. Squares, every square from a tiny 3×3
  to an 8×8. Specials, every other size from a 3×4 mini to an 8×10. And Gallery Grade, the art
  papers.
- **A page for every print.** Each size has its own page saying what it is, where it goes and what
  it's for, with a small viewer you can drag to turn the print in the light.
- **Plain names.** Prints go by their size, like "4×6", and the longer-lasting versions are called
  Archival.
- **Paper and finish at a glance.** Each line lists its paper and its finishes beside its prints.
- **Gallery Grade is coming soon.** Seven art papers, from smooth matte to textured cotton, and two
  metallic foils, silver and gold.
- **Drawn cards.** The front page shows a beam of light from a 3×3 slide onto an 8×8 print, packs
  of prints on a black desk, and a fan of every art paper.
- **The same everywhere.** The site looks and works the same in Safari as in Chrome, and the
  default look follows your device's light or dark mode.

## 0.6: The editor · October 2026

The photo editor is finished. What you see on screen is what gets printed.

- **Your prints at their true sizes.** A 4×6 and an 8×10 sit side by side in proportion, so you
  can see what you're ordering.
- **Edit one photo or many.** Change a single print, a group of the same size, or a whole page at
  once, with Undo, Redo and Reset.
- **Positioning.** Drag a photo on the print with thirds lines to line it up, then zoom, rotate or
  mirror it. Fill covers the print; Fit shows the whole photo.
- **Borders and captions.** Classic, Instant and Gallery borders in any colour. Captions typed
  straight onto the print, on the photo or in the border, in ten typefaces.
- **Light & Colour.** Exposure, contrast, warmth and more, with presets shown on your own photo and
  a button to compare with the original.
- **Quality.** Each photo is graded for the size you picked, with plain advice, and a one-tap
  smaller size when a photo isn't sharp enough.
- **Turn the print in your hand.** A simulated print catches the light like real paper, and shows
  your photo's true colours when it's still.
- **Quick with hundreds of photos.** Drop in whole folders and RAW files; photos get ready in the
  background while you work.
- **Your photos stay yours.** Each photo keeps its camera details while you edit, never its
  location, and none of it goes with your order.
- **The first catalogue.** Prints in two shops, The Classics and Squares & Specials, and Fine Art
  papers, each size drawn to scale.
- Works on phones, comes in several themes, and a reload keeps your place.
